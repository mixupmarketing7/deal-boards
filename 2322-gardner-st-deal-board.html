02Four gates before any money moves

1. ZoningThe lot is 50 ft wide and the zone asks for 75 ft. Get a written letter from Augusta Planning (706-821-1796) that a house can be permitted. The zoning rewrite heard Wed 10/21 may help.
2. BuilderTwo fixed-price bids from licensed builders at $194,000 or less.
3. LenderA lender approves the builder and the one-time-close loan, with cash to close at $30,000 or less.
4. AppraisalThe "subject to" appraisal comes in at about $207,000 or more, before the loan closes.

If any gate fails, walk. The lot offer carries a 60-day due-diligence period and refundable earnest money, so only the time is at risk.
