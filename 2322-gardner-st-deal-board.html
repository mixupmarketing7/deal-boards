The lot is about 50 ft wide, and R-1B asks for 75 ft. That is the first gate, and it costs nothing to test.

The zoning rewrite — hearing Wed 10/21/2026, 2 PM

The Planning Commission holds a special hearing on the new zoning code on 10/21. The Augusta Commission vote date and the effective date are not published. In the 10/14 draft, R-1B becomes SSR (Standard Suburban Residential): 6,000 sf area, 70 ft width, 35% coverage, 25 ft front, 20 ft rear, 5 ft sides.

How it helps

§8.1.2(B): a lot below the minimum area or width "may accommodate any use allowed in its zoning district." §5.13.3 infill: on an old narrow lot, "existing lot area, lot width, and lot dimensions may remain," with smaller setbacks and no coverage cap. Accessory units become allowed with an administrative permit: owner must live there, 500 sf maximum on lots of 1 acre or less.

How it could hurt

It is unclear which code applies to a permit filed during the switch. The infill bonus needs more than half the block face built before 2000; this block is about half vacant, so that is borderline. The draft can still change after 10/21.

Does the lot count toward his down payment? Yes. FHA counts land equity toward his 3.5%. A lot owned less than 6 months counts at the lower of cost or appraised value.
