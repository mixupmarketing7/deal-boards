Verdict — Brookstone and Deerfield, as live-in buys

Six Atlanta listings

5770 Deerfield Trl · 1174 Brookstone Rd · 141 Walnut St SW · 2702 Rollingwood Ln SE · 1761 Liberty Pkwy NW · 2453 Ivydale Dr SW. Each one tested two ways: as a buy-and-hold rental, and as a home the buyer lives in for a year, then rents out and steps up from.

Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and seller analysis. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for any listing agent or seller.

None of the six works as a pure rental at today's prices and rates. Two work as live-in step-ups: 1174 Brookstone Rd and 5770 Deerfield Trl. A third, 1761 Liberty Pkwy, works only if its association allows leasing.

Worked example, 1174 Brookstone. A bank-approved short sale at $215,000, worth about $235,000. With FHA at 3.5% down, cash to close is about $16,975, and living there costs about $1,902 a month against $1,790 to rent it. After the buyer moves on, the rent leaves about $603 a month to cover, and equity at month 24 is about $38,000. As a pure rental the same house returns 4.6% on cost, short of the 7% target.
