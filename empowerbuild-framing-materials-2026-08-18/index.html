Supplier comparison

Menards

The same four direct product pages total $1,198.10. The 2×8×10 line was not available at Indianapolis East, so it is not a complete local pickup basket. The 11% rebate is future store credit, not an immediate checkout reduction.

Home Depot

Three exact lines were locally priced and stocked. The localized 2×4×8 page did not return a dependable Post Road price, so no unsupported all-Home-Depot total is shown.

Lowe’s

No Indianapolis grand total is supportable. Three product responses reverted to Atlanta #1875, and the identified 2×4×8 SKU was unpublished/not buyable.

Before buying

  1. Confirm that 7/16-inch, 24/16 span-rated OSB complies with the plans, support spacing, and fastening schedule.
  2. Confirm the Paslode nailer model, collation angle, nail length, diameter, coating, and intended application. Eighteen-gauge brads are finish fasteners, not structural framing nails.
  3. Ask the Home Depot Pro Desk to quote the complete 160-unit package and compare its delivered total against $1,095.70.
  4. Recheck pricing, quantity tiers, stock, tax, and delivery immediately before approval.
