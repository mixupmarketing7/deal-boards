01 · Context

County valuation work has two audiences: the model and the reviewer.

A county team does not need another opaque score. It needs to know what changed, why the system is recommending it, what evidence supports the recommendation, which issues still need attention, and whether the result is ready to move into production.

Operational challenge

Recommendations were not enough.

Revaluation work spans parcel records, neighborhood factors, improvement models, land schedules, sales evidence, ratio studies, data exceptions, and board communication. The product had to connect those layers without asking an assessor to trust a black box.

Design opportunity

Turn analysis into a reviewable decision.

The workbench makes the recommendation, evidence, confidence, risk, action, and export state visible together. Every important output has a human checkpoint.

02 · Discovery

Research shaped the workflow around how county teams decide.

1

Start with the executive question

What changed across the county, what is the likely assessment impact, and where should the team spend review time first?

2

Move from countywide signal to prioritized queue

Rank recommendations by risk, confidence, reason code, data quality, and review status. Preserve a compact queue for daily work.

3

Open the parcel only when context is needed

Show current assessment, modeled value, recommended value, plain-language rationale, source evidence, and the assessor’s decision controls.

4

Close the loop with board-ready delivery

Compare scenarios, resolve data exceptions, capture decisions, and generate a controlled export with readiness and lineage.

03 · Product architecture

Six surfaces, one governed decision path.

01 · Prioritize

Executive dashboard

Countywide recommendation summary, digest delta, tax impact, confidence, and export readiness in one opening view.

02 · Triage

Review queue

Decision-first parcel rows with class, neighborhood, current and recommended values, risk, reason, and next action.

03 · Explain

Parcel evidence

Plain-language rationale beside the source components that produced the recommendation, with accept, defer, and override actions.

04 · Compare

Scenario comparison

Median ratio, COD, PRD, digest delta, tax impact, implementation risk, and export readiness before board approval.

05 · Protect

Data-quality review

Missing condition, invalid square footage, model disagreement, insufficient comps, and suspicious ratios are visible before delivery.

06 · Deliver

Board pack and export

Recommendation rationale, risks, compliance scorecard, implementation notes, row counts, checksums, and production-safe status.

06 · Interview framing

“I designed the review system around trust.”

Short version: “I translated county revaluation analysis into an explainable assessor workflow. The product connected executive signals, prioritized parcel review, source evidence, scenario comparison, data-quality gates, board communication, and controlled export. The key principle was that a recommendation is only useful when a human can understand it, challenge it, and account for what happens next.”
