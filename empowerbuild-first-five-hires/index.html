C-Suite hiring session
August 15, 2026 · Advisory under CF-009
Executive TL;DR

Build the control spine before adding growth payroll.

EmpowerBuild’s first five seats should establish financial truth and daily ownership first, protect field delivery second, and add sales and preconstruction capacity only after the numbers support it.

The decision
Five seats. Not five immediate hires.

Seats 1–2 are stabilization roles. Seats 3–5 are conditional and require verified workload, cash, capacity, and operating discipline.

Hiring waves

Scale only after the prior gate is working.

The C-suite rejected a conventional hiring spree because current cash, signed backlog, margin at completion, capacity, and staffing economics remain unverified.

Wave A
Stabilize

Start with finance control and operating coordination. Establish source-linked truth and remove recurring clerical work from Alvin and Caleb.

Wave B
Protect delivery

Add field leadership after active jobs, contracts, schedules, crew coverage, cash, and margins are recertified.

Wave C
Convert capacity

Add a closer after the pipeline is clean and operations can state what work it can deliver without bypassing controls.

Wave D
Scale preconstruction

Add dedicated bid and trade coordination only when recurring workload proves a sustained bottleneck.

Decision controls

What must be true before payroll expands

Affordability and workload gates

  • Four timely, reconciled weekly finance packets
  • A human-reviewed 13-week cash forecast
  • Verified contracts, deposits, billing status, and margin data
  • Current project, crew, and four-week trade-capacity records
  • A written role scope, decision rights, and 90-day scorecard

Explicitly deferred

  • Full-time CFO
  • COO / integrator executive
  • VP of Sales
  • CTO / automation engineer
  • HR manager / recruiter
  • Marketing manager / paid lead generation
  • In-house legal or compliance executive
  • Chief estimator
  • Procurement executive
  • Broad field-crew expansion
