The finding that matters most

You are being shown the retail end of a lot-flipping pipeline

Gaines Capital Investments LLC — owner of 542 Basin St and four Turpin Hill lots — bought 26 Gregg St for $3,500 on 2024-12-06. The same parcel transferred at $18,000 on 2024-12-17. Eleven days.

The model: acquire tax-sale and Land Bank dirt at $2,500–$3,500, resell to the builders actively developing the block at $12,900–$18,000. Both parcels in this fleet sit downstream of that trade.

The better trade is upstream. The Augusta Georgia Land Bank Authority holds 27 vacant lots in Turpin Hill alone and disposes at nominal prices — 33 Gregg came out of it at $2,500 and became a $215,000 new build.

36 Gregg St, 30901 — conditional

Buildable dirt with a title problem

PIN 059-3-476-00-0 · 3,885 sf · zoned R-1C · FEMA Zone X (minimal hazard) · taxes delinquent 1 yr

Buildability is proven, not assumed. 30 Gregg and 33 Gregg are both 0.09-acre lots carrying new 1,316 sf houses built in 2025 — identical lot size to the subject. The street is platted at 0.09 ac throughout and carries houses from 1906, 1916, 1934 and 1953. Augusta permits building on these substandard lots of record.

Title is the entire question. Three to four fractional heir interests, no arm's-length transfer in the county record, dormant since 1994, owners absentee in metro Atlanta, one year of delinquent tax. Georgia's Uniform Partition of Heirs Property Act applies.
