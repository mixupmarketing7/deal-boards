Internal working document. Built from MLS agent sheets and public records for Alvin Slate and deal partners. Not for the listing agent or the seller.

This home cannot be a rental, and an investor cannot buy it. It is an Atlanta Land Trust unit. The buyer must qualify by income and live in it, the land is leased instead of sold, and renting is barred for any length of time.

Worked example. Suppose the rules did not exist. A new one-bedroom here rents for about $1,200 a month. After vacancy, management, repairs, taxes, insurance and $1,200 a year in association dues, it nets $5,500 to $7,200 a year. Against a cost of about $170,900 at the ask, that is a return of 3.2% to 4.2%. A 7% target would need an all-in cost of $78,000 to $103,000. The price fails before the restriction does.
