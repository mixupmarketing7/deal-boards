Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

Two one-bedroom units cannot carry $300,000. And because a duplex is no longer allowed here, the second unit may already be gone legally if it has sat empty a year.

Worked example. Buy at $300,000, spend $15,291 to make it rent-ready and carry it two months: total cost $331,203. Two units at $950 each net $8,838 a year, a 2.7% return. The red-team adds owner-paid water, a leasing fee and a real reserve, and net income falls to $4,595, a 1.4% return. Even at the Atlanta Housing voucher rent of $1,250 a unit, the 7% price is $129,712.
