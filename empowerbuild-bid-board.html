To work with us

What we need from youLo que necesitamos de usted

Nothing unusual, but we do not schedule or pay anyone until all four are on file. Getting them in early is what puts you at the front of the list.

A completed W-9Formulario W-9 completo

Business name and EIN as they appear with the IRS.

Certificate of insuranceCertificado de seguro

General liability and workers' compensation, current, with us named as certificate holder. Required limits are stated in the bid package you receive when you register.

Three referencesTres referencias

Recent work, with a contact who will actually pick up the phone. A general contractor you have worked under is worth more than a homeowner.

Signed subcontractor agreementContrato de subcontratista firmado

We send it after your documents clear. It covers scope, payment, insurance, safety and close-out. Read it — we would rather answer questions before you sign than after.
