01 · The brief

The problem was not just valuation. It was decision confidence.

PRE work crossed several high-stakes moments: deciding whether a property was worth pursuing, assembling county evidence, explaining a recommendation, preparing for a hearing, and delivering a defensible result. The operating reality was fragmented evidence, single-number savings estimates, manual county values, and rework when a case was refreshed.

Homeowner appeal work

Make the case worth filing, then make the result legible.

Analysts needed comps, official assessment values, filing deadlines, levy context, and outcome recording in one workflow. The design response was to show evidence components and ranges rather than hide uncertainty behind a single rank or savings number.

Government revaluation work

Make model recommendations reviewable by an assessor.

The government-services concept needed parcel evidence, reason codes, confidence, scenario comparison, data-quality gates, board-ready recommendations, and an export trail. A recommendation was only useful if a human could explain, override, defer, or reject it.

02 · Research to product

Discovery changed the shape of the product.

Observe the actual analyst workflow

Deadline and workflow state mattered more than an internal pursuit rank. Evidence rework was the felt pain: match confirmations, comp selections, corrective documents, addresses, and official values did not consistently survive reruns.

Separate evidence from interpretation

County-record FMV, class, tax district, valid sales, PRE evidence, and analyst decisions should sit beside one another. Tyler or county data could inform the review, but should never silently auto-apply.

Replace false precision with bounded ranges

DeKalb homestead status was not available. The resulting design called for a two-scenario savings range with a visible “homestead unknown” state, keyed to tax district rather than postal city.

Make delivery a governed action

Government recommendations move through review, data-quality checks, decision capture, board-pack generation, and controlled export. The audit trail is part of the product, not an afterthought.

05 · What was designed

A connected service, not a collection of screens.

Analyst workbench

Review queue

Prioritize by deadline, evidence, confidence, reason code, and review action. Keep the decision surface compact and operational.

Evidence system

Parcel evidence

Show current, modeled, and recommended values beside plain-language rationale and source evidence. Preserve overrides and audit actions.

Decision support

Scenario comparison

Compare median ratio, COD, PRD, digest delta, tax impact, implementation risk, and export readiness before board approval.

Quality control

Data-quality gate

Expose missing condition, invalid square footage, model disagreement, insufficient comps, and suspicious ratios before production export.

Government delivery

Board recommendation pack

Turn analysis into an executive summary with rationale, risks, compliance scorecard, implementation notes, and lineage.

Controlled output

Export manifest

Track row counts, checksums, readiness, and production-safe status for WinGap parcel recommendations and related reports.

Frameworks were used to change decisions.

Claims-safe evidence designConsumer savings and government recommendationsUse ranges, provenance, “homestead unknown,” and “confirm against the county bill” rather than promise eligibility or savings.

Service blueprint thinkingAppeal journey and county deliveryConnect intake, analysis, review, hearing preparation, decision capture, board communication, and export into one service path.
What I would not claim: the available evidence does not establish a completed Prosci/ADKAR or Lean Six Sigma implementation. The defensible claim is applied human-centered product and service design with explicit evidence governance.
