How I work

I move on information, not instinct. That has been the job for sixteen years.

Seven of those were at Cox Automotive. I owned dealer self-service and inventory data as a Senior Product Owner, then ran dealer sales analytics as Senior Manager of Business Intelligence. Pricing data car shoppers had to trust. Reporting a sales organization ran on every day.

Since 2023 I have been Co-Founder and COO of Property Revaluation Experts, a property valuation platform serving homeowners and county government clients. I build the AI tooling myself, using Claude, Cursor, and Replit. Writing the spec and shipping the system are the same job for me.

Alongside it I run a real estate practice, a fractional COO seat at a construction company, a licensed mortgage lending practice, and a marketing shop. Same approach every time: own the data, build the system, ship it myself.
