Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

The rent cannot carry it at any price this seller will take. It needs a price near $72,000 to return 7%, and the house is worth about $140,000 as it sits.

Worked example. Buy at $174,900, spend $46,495 on repairs and carry it three and a half months: total cost $235,736. At $1,950 a month it nets $11,180 a year, a 4.7% return against a 7% target. A lender sizing the loan to the rent lends $100,975, leaving $136,780 in the house. The red-team's corrections (rent $1,800, a leasing fee, a larger reserve) cut the return to 3.5%.
