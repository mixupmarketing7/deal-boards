Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

The rent cannot carry this house. At $324,900 it is a fair price for a family that will live in it and the wrong price for a landlord. No purchase price the bank would accept fixes that.

Worked example. Buy at the ask, spend $121,349 on repairs, and carry it four months. The total cost is $476,439. It rents for about $2,850 a month. After vacancy, management, repairs, a $9,161 tax bill and insurance, it nets $11,988 a year. That is a return of 2.5% on cost against a 7% target. A lender who sizes the loan to the rent will lend $114,812, which leaves $363,924 of your cash in the house. With the best rent, the lowest repair budget and a lower tax value all at once, the return reaches 4.0%. The red-team's corrections cut the base return to 1.6% and push both price tests below zero.
