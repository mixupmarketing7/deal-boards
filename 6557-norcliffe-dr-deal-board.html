Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

The rent cannot carry this house. At $324,900 it is a fair price for a family that will live in it and the wrong price for a landlord. No purchase price the bank would accept fixes that.

Worked example. Buy at the ask, spend $121,349 on repairs, and carry it four months. The total cost is $476,439. It rents for about $2,850 a month. After vacancy, management, repairs, a $9,161 tax bill and insurance, it nets $11,988 a year. That is a return of 2.5% on cost against a 7% target. A lender who sizes the loan to the rent will lend $114,812, which leaves $363,924 of your cash in the house. With the best rent, the lowest repair budget and a lower tax bill all at once, the return reaches 4.4%.

10The seller — the leverage is theirs

A loan servicer that already ran a bidding round

Lakeview Loan Servicing took the house back through a VA-loan foreclosure at $390,832. The county values it at $486,600. It listed at $324,900 and called for highest-and-best within a week. The automated estimates ($322,100 to $332,684) only echo the list price.

The ask is already at as-is value

True value as it sits is about $335,000, in a range of $295,000 to $360,000. An owner-occupant can meet that. A rental buyer tops out at $37,076 on return and below zero on the refinance test. There is no meeting point.

Terms that close doors

No assignments and no seller financing. A servicer gives no disclosure. Utilities may be off, which blocks inspection of the heat and plumbing until someone asks for them to be turned on.
