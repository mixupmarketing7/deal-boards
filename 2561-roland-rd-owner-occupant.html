Gate 2 — sewage, and therefore financing

The MLS lists sewer as “Holding Tank” — a pump-out vessel, not a permanent waste system for a three-bedroom dwelling under Georgia DPH rules. The county Permits module is empty: no septic permit, no soil record, nothing in 51 years of ownership.

FHA, VA and USDA RD all require a permitted system acceptable to the local health authority, and most conventional lenders do too. No permittable system means no financing — on your purchase and on any future sale. USDA Rural Development, which is otherwise built for exactly this property, is unavailable until this clears.

Cost if permittable: $18,000 standard install. $25,000–$40,000 if soils fail or lake setbacks and Soil Productivity 4 force an engineered / advanced-treatment system — plus perc testing and county permitting time.

Financing paths if the tank cannot be cured before closing:

  • FHA 203(k) or Fannie Mae HomeStyle Renovation — finances purchase and the septic install together
  • Local Upson / Middle-Georgia bank portfolio or construction-to-perm loan
  • Cash, cure the system, then rate-and-term refinance

That is a mass-appraisal land schedule moving, not a market — and it is why this board does not use the county's $252,101 as evidence of value.
