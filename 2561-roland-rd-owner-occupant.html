The blocker: sewage, and therefore financing

The MLS lists sewer as “Holding Tank.” That is not a conforming septic system for a three-bedroom house. FHA, VA and USDA all require the sewage system be acceptable to the local health authority; Georgia does not generally permit holding tanks as permanent residential sewage disposal. If it cannot be made conforming, conventional and government financing both fail at appraisal and the buyer pool collapses to cash and portfolio lenders.

That is the seller's problem as much as yours, and it is your single largest piece of leverage. It is also why USDA Rural Development — 0% down, and this is exactly the rural profile it exists for — is unavailable until the sewage question clears.

Realistic financing paths if the tank cannot be cured before closing:

  • FHA 203(k) or Fannie Mae HomeStyle Renovation — finances the purchase and the septic install together
  • Local Upson/Middle-GA bank portfolio or construction-to-perm loan
  • Cash purchase, cure the system, then rate-and-term refinance
