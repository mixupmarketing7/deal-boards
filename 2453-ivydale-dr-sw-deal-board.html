Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

A bank-owned, half-finished, unpermitted conversion sitting in a flood plain. The rent cannot carry it, and the flood rules may stop the work entirely.

Worked example. Buy at $229,900, spend $86,902 to finish it and carry it four months: total cost $338,389. As a 4-bedroom at $2,000 a month it nets $8,823 a year, a 2.6% return. The finished house is worth about $210,000. The red-team's corrections push the full-refinance price to −$9,422, and a likely flood premium takes the 7% price down to $6,807.
