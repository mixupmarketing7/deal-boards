Sheriff Registration — This Week's Errand (verified 2026-08-02; full runbook in underwrites/govease-registration-runbook.md)

  • Choose the bidding entity — which LLC signs. Every document below keys off this one decision. Out-of-state entity? Foreign registration required first (~$95, no SLA — start immediately).
  • Create GovEase account (govease.com) and register for "IN – Marion Sheriff Sales." No cash deposit to bid. All paperwork due Fri Aug 14, 3:00 PM ET — register in the next 2–3 days, MCSO approval time is unpublished
  • Pull two INBiz documents (~$20 each, online, same-day): unexpired Business Entity Report copy + fresh Certificate of Existence.
  • Two phone calls before e-signing: MCSO 317-327-2459 — their W-9 rule says SSN only, no FEIN, all bidders, which conflicts with standard entity W-9 practice; and GovEase 769-208-5050 — where "Deed Information" (deed vesting + mailing address) gets captured.
  • E-sign four forms in GovEase: Bidder Acknowledgment, Business Connections (list every entity affiliation — the no-delinquency affidavit is perjury-backed and flows through entities), W-9, Purchase Agreement. Upload signer's photo ID.
  • Enter Requested Approval Amount — your total spending cap; must equal funds you can actually deliver. Final by Thu Aug 20, noon ET
  • If you win: sales disclosure by Tue Aug 25; cashier's check in full, payable to "Marion County Sheriff's Office," in the bidder's registered name, by Wed Aug 26, 3:00 PM. Default = lifetime MCSO ban + statutory resale-deficiency liability.

Legal spine (Indiana Code, verified): no owner redemption after the sale (IC 32-29-7-9(b)); owners can redeem any time before it, so the list shrinks daily; liens not in the lawsuit survive — the -PL-/-CC- cause trap; occupancy/eviction is entirely the buyer's problem; title search every target.
