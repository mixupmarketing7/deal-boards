Internal working document. Contains maximum offers, walk-away prices and an analysis of the seller and listing agent. For Alvin Slate and deal partners only. Not for the listing agent, the seller, or anyone on the other side of this deal.

A new house here costs more to build than it sells for. The lot price is not the problem. In this pocket a finished new home sells for about $211 a square foot. Building one costs about $278 a square foot, all in.

Worked example. A 1,750 sf, 3-bed/2.5-bath spec house (the smallest plan R-75 allows by right) sells for about $370,000. After 6% commission and 2.5% buyer concessions, that nets $338,550. Buying the lot at the ask and building takes 14 months and costs $486,583. The result is a loss of $148,033. With the lot free, the same build still loses $50,620. The red-team's corrections deepen the loss to $312,638.

03Market read — ASRE warehouse, queried 13 Sep 2026

Four signals. The one that looks bullish is a composition effect.

a · Values are falling; list prices only look like they're rising

The Zillow home value index for 30032 is $239,082 (Jul 2026). That is −6.0% year over year and −20.9% below its Aug 2022 peak. The FHFA ZIP index fell 4.16% in 2025 while DeKalb County rose 0.69%. The Realtor.com median list price rose 16.7% to $315,000, but only because bigger houses listed. Median listing size went from 1,380 to 1,621 sf, while list $/sf moved only $187 → $197.

b · Demand is thin

In Aug 2026, 30.1% of 30032 listings had a price cut. The pending ratio is 0.19. Listing page views run 0.30× the US average, and the ZIP ranks 11,055th on Realtor.com hotness (demand score 3.8 of 100). Redfin labels 30032 a buyer's market at a 95.8% sale-to-list ratio.

c · New construction is a thin, slow slice of this ZIP

New builds were 13 of 556 single-family sales in the last 12 months (2.3%). They carry an estimated 8.3 months of supply vs 6.1 for resales. The median new-build sale is $609,000, driven by larger product in the northern pockets near East Lake. Only four new builds closed at $400K or less. The one nearest this lot, 2127 Nichols Ln, was listed at $549,000 in Jan 2025. Over 454 days it was pulled twice and briefly advertised for rent, then sold for $351,000 (−36%).

d · The buyer pool can't carry the price

At 6.71% (30-yr, FRED 9/3/2026) a $350,000 FHA purchase runs $2,916–$3,210 a month. That requires $89,753–$124,261 of household income, against a 30032 median of $62,765 (ACS 2024). Down-payment help (Georgia Dream up to $10,000; the DeKalb bond program up to $20,000, per news reports) lowers cash to close, not the payment.

a · It's the strongest evidence against the verdict

The bigger house cuts the loss at likely costs from −$148,033 (1,750 sf) to −$109,454. A volume builder gets close to even.

b · It still doesn't work for a one-off builder

The twin is unsold at $599,000 after a cut. 2307 itself needed two relists and a $10K buydown, and its closing concessions aren't known. Houses of 2,200 sf and up in 30032 carry about 9.8 months of supply.

c · It names the realistic buyer for the lot

A production builder putting up ~3,000 sf at ~$110/sf breaks even on land near $60,000. That is who would buy this dirt, and it is consistent with the $55,000 resale assumed in exit C.

d · It cuts against two red-team corrections

A 14-month lot-to-sale cycle was achieved 0.5 mi away, which weakens the 19-month schedule correction. A builder paid $90,000 for a lot in May 2025, which supports the agents' $72K–$80K land value more than the red-team's $55K. That sale predates the last 12 months of weaker lot trades.

e · Fit on this lot

A 3,000 sf two-story needs about a 1,500 sf footprint plus a garage. It fits easily if Ellen Way is the front. If Ousley Ct is the front, the largest rectangle in the wedge is about 2,018 sf, so it's tight. A $30 zoning certification decides which reading applies.
