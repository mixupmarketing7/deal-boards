Steel-manning the bull case

Three things that are genuinely true

1. Vertical integration. In-house crews and wholesale material take 35–45% off retail GC pricing — a $340K trade subtotal becomes ~$200–220K. Real.
2. Scope discipline. The kitchen and one bath appear salvageable in the photos; refinish rather than replace floors; repair rather than swap windows. Another $40–60K.
3. It may not be a gut. If structure and roof are sound, the job is demo/haul, rewire, replumb, HVAC, drywall, kitchen, two baths, floors, paint — genuinely ~$200K with your own crews.

What that best case actually pays

At $200K rehab, $38K carry, 9 months: −$20,000 at base ARV, +$44,750 at $580K — a 9.1% return on cost for 9–12 months on an uninsurable, unfinanceable, historic-district gut where the two largest cost lines are unverified. Below what a stabilized rental returns, with far more risk. And the downside is not symmetrical: if structure and roof are compromised, the same purchase loses $120–270K.

Not yet verified

Historic district status. The parcel is almost certainly inside Atlanta's West End Historic District (Code Ch. 20G) and possibly SPI-21. If so, AUDC must issue a Certificate of Appropriateness before any exterior work, window replacement may be forced to repair-in-kind, and demolition of a contributing 1930 structure is restricted — which means the land floor may not be reachable.

The ADU claim is unverified. "35×142 lot can allow an ADU" is marketing, not entitlement. On a 35–41 ft frontage under R5 setbacks, treat as false until surveyed. Do not pay for it.

Insurability. Builder's risk on a structure carrying an unsafe-property waiver is frequently declined or surcharged — confirm before arranging hard money.

Permits at exit. Unpermitted exterior work in a historic district draws a stop-work order before the appraisal. The $510K retail buyer needs a clean permit history to finance.

Comp renovation quality is inferred. Redfin detail pages are CloudFront-blocked, so the $212–$246/sf cohort was priced as "renovated" from price and vintage, not confirmed from photos. If several were light cosmetic flips, base ARV falls further.
